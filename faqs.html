Questions & Comments

If you have any feedback for us, or would like to leave a question, you can do it here: Questions & Comments .

Q1: What are the dates for Gospel Study Classes?

Classes start September 3 and go through November 19, 2026. (We will post a new schedule for 2027)

Q2: What days and times are Gospel Study Classes?

Every Thursday at 7:00 PM.

Q3: Do I have to go to the same class the whole time?

No! Find a class that interests you and join the class that feels right for you.

Q4: How can I contact the North Utah Committee?

Either leave a question or comment through the website, or reach out on the True North Facebook Page .
