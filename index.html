About Us

Purpose and Mission

Our focus as disciples of Christ, who also happen to be single adults in Northern Utah, is to Find Our People and Follow Our Purpose as we look to our True North, Jesus Christ.

We strive to develop faith in Jesus Christ, make covenants with Him, and build a strong community through wholesome activities.

We seek to gather as righteous sons and daughters of God and find strength in faithful discipleship.
