About President Kevin Eubank

Kevin M. Eubank was born and raised in Bountiful, Utah, and is the 6th of seven children born to Mark & Jean Eubank. He graduated from the University of Utah with a bachelor's degree in broadcast meteorology, and is a member of both the American Meteorological Society and the National Weather Association. He has worked as a broadcast meteorologist for over two decades and is currently the Chief Meteorologist for KSL Television in Salt Lake City, Utah.

He served his mission in the Mexico Chihuahua Mission, and has served in a variety of callings in the Church. He is currently serving as the Stake President for the Bountiful Utah Heights Stake.

President Eubank met his wife Jana Holbrook Eubank at Bountiful High School, where they were both students. They were married in the Bountiful Temple and have three daughters; Jensyn, Maryn, and Camryn, and one son, Owen.

President Eubank enjoys working on projects. You’ll often find him remodeling, fixing sprinklers, or repairing something that’s broken. He also loves the outdoors! He mountain bikes, wakeboards, and has snowboarded since he was a young boy.
