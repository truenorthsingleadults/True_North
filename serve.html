Service

"Searching after and Rescuing the One, while ministering faithfully to the Ninety-Nine."

Granite Education Foundation

The mission of the Granite Education Foundation is to reduce barriers to learning and increase access to opportunities for students and educators within the Granite School District in Salt Lake County.

By engaging business and community partners, the foundation provides crucial resources—such as food assistance, student aid, and educator support—to improve academic success, student well-being, and overall classroom equity.

This community-driven effort includes hands-on volunteer initiatives, such as tying handmade fleece blankets to send home with students to help ensure they stay warm, comfortable, and supported.

Our Service Project

As part of our True North Kickoff Social, we will be tying 250 fleece blankets to help support the Granite Education Foundation and the students they serve. Details for this project are found on the Connect Page.
